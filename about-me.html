About me

Research, education and beyond

I’m Giuseppe, an Italian scholar based in Switzerland. I moved here to pursue my PhD after training in economics, a field that still shapes how I think about evidence, decisions, and the way people respond to information. Over time, my path has moved through universities in different roles: student, researcher, lecturer, tutor, and contributor to academic service.

Those experiences taught me to read institutions from the inside, to listen carefully before drawing conclusions, and to enjoy the slow work of understanding context. I am continuing my research projects while looking for a role in research or higher education.

Away from work, I read often, sometimes philosophically and often across languages, because I am drawn to questions that take time to unfold. I’m also an experimental cook, a traveler when schedules allow, and a devoted listener of good music.
