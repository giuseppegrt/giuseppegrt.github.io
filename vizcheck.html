Working alpha · Partner program

A second pair of eyes for your charts.

VizCheck is a research-informed app in development for people who communicate with data. Create, check and refine charts with practical guidance.

Read on to see how it works and how you can help shape it. Interested in partnering? Get in touch below.

Create, check and refine

  1. Bring your data

    Paste spreadsheet cells, import a CSV or TSV, or start with an example. Build a supported chart and preview changes.

  2. Check your choices

    Spot missing labels, units or context. Compare alternatives and choose what to change.

  3. Keep control

    Undo edits, save your project and export charts and review notes. Editorial judgment stays with you.

Practical checks, informed choices

The diagnostic reviews the chart's recorded design and supplied information, then suggests what to inspect or improve. It cannot verify source data, certify publication quality or guarantee an audience's response.

Developed by Giuseppe Gruttad'Auria, VizCheck aims to bring visualization research into everyday chart-making.

VizCheck is a work in progress. Some features use fictional examples to show how the app works, rather than provide validated predictions. The app's guidance will evolve alongside my research, incorporating new findings as they emerge.

Help shape VizCheck

If you make or use charts, your experience can help. Individuals and teams are welcome; no specialist training is needed.

  • Bring a task. Share what you need from charts in your work.
  • Try and discuss. Explore an early version by arrangement, using non-sensitive examples.
  • Return and refine. Test later improvements and help set priorities.

This is an ongoing conversation, with follow-up testing agreed together.
